The Product

Two varieties.
Nine months
of supply.

W. Murcott (Class I, from the Huaral Valley) opens our season in April and runs to July. Malvacea, a conventionally grown seeded variety, follows from August to December — one supply partner from April through December.

Both varieties are exported under SENASA registration with a phytosanitary certificate per shipment, packed in 10 kg or 15 kg cartons and shipped by sea in 40-ft reefer containers. Satsuma, Tango and Orri are available on request.

Why Good Hope Mandarins

Two varieties.
One supply
partner.

Buyers running a citrus program need continuity. W. Murcott covers April to July and Malvacea picks up from August to December, so one supplier relationship can carry you through nine months.

Fruit is packed in 10 kg or 15 kg cartons and shipped by sea in 40-ft reefer containers. The cold chain follows each destination's protocol, including cold treatment for the USA.

Tell us your caliber mix, destination and weekly program, and we will confirm availability and quote FOB Callao, CFR or CIF.
