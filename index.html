Who We Are

A certified supply
partner rooted in
Peruvian origin.

Good Hope Produce exports certified organic Ginger and Turmeric, plus fresh Mandarins and Goldenberries, from Peru's growing regions — delivering traceable, export-ready supply to importers, distributors and industrial buyers in regulated markets.

Every shipment travels with a phytosanitary certificate and lot-level traceability. Ginger and turmeric add organic certification (USDA, EU and Canada), GlobalG.A.P., GRASP and lab analysis by Groen Agro Control.

We respond to procurement inquiries within one business day.
