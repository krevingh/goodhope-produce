The Product

Fresh organic ginger.
Graded to
your spec.

Good Hope Produce exports fresh organic ginger of the Criolla (yellow) variety, grown in Peru. Roots are size-graded into four calibers — 50–100 g, 101–150 g, 151–250 g and 250 g+ — and packed in 13.6 kg (30 lb) cartons or 8 kg baskets, or to your own format.

Fresh root is our core program; dried ginger is available on request. Every lot is analysed by Groen Agro Control and ships with organic certificates (USDA, EU and Canada), GlobalG.A.P. and GRASP documentation, and a phytosanitary certificate.

"For industrial buyers, the question is never whether ginger tastes like ginger — it's whether it tastes the same every shipment."

Why Good Hope Ginger

Consistency your
program can
rely on.

Importers and distributors sourcing ginger need the same thing every week: predictable sizing, reliable volumes and documentation that clears customs the first time.

We ship from 2.5 t per week by air and from 20 t up to 40 t per week by sea, in 40-ft reefer containers or on skids. Lead time is 5 days for air orders and 7 days for sea orders.

Tell us your caliber mix, packaging and destination, and we will quote FOB Callao, CFR or CIF.
