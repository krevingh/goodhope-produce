The Product

Peruvian Palillo.
Certified organic
fresh turmeric.

Good Hope Produce exports fresh organic turmeric (Curcuma longa) of the Palillo variety. Roots are sized to customer request within agreed weight tolerances and packed for each destination: 13.6 kg or 9 kg boxes for the EU, 30 lb boxes for Canada.

Fresh root is our core program; dried turmeric is available on request. Every lot is analysed by Groen Agro Control and ships with organic certificates (USDA, EU and Canada), GlobalG.A.P. and GRASP documentation, and a phytosanitary certificate.

Why Good Hope Turmeric

Your quality control
starts before the
shipment arrives.

Procurement teams sourcing turmeric need predictable supply and documentation that holds up to audit. Our program is built around both.

We ship from 2.5 t per week by air and from 20 t by sea in 40-ft reefer containers, up to 30 t per week, held at 11–12.5 °C. Lead time is 5 days for air orders and 7 days for sea orders.

Every lot is analysed by Groen Agro Control and documented end to end, so your QA team has what it needs before the container lands.
